How Blind Poker Fury plays

Texas hold'em with the lights off. You bet on a feeling, then the dealer lays out every card and the table finds out together.

  1. Blinds and the blind bet. Small and big blind post (plus a straddle if it's switched on). Everyone bets, calls, raises, or folds before any cards are dealt.
  2. The deal. Two hole cards to each player still in, face down. Burn, flop, burn, turn, burn, river. The dealer's job is done.
  3. Fury rules: show one. Every player picks their left or right card, and they all flip at the same time. Then one more round of betting.
  4. Blind rules: no second round. Left cards flip on their own, straight to the reveal.
  5. The reveal. Three, two, one, and the last cards turn over. Best five-card hand from two hole cards plus the board takes it. Anyone all-in shows their left card automatically.

Braille and voice

Switch it on in Table setup. Every card face shows braille instead of print, rank on top and suit in the middle, and the table talks: the deal, each reveal, the countdown, and who won.

  • Keyboard: F fold, C check or call, R raise, A all-in, L or ← show left, → show right, H hear the table, N next hand.
  • Screen readers get a spoken label on every card, so a refreshable braille display reads real tactile braille.
  • On-screen braille dots are visual. They can't be felt on glass by themselves.
