Pick your table

Blackjack Reversal

The original table. Bet whatever you're comfortable with against an 8-deck shoe, and once a hand you can call a Reversal — the dealer flips their hole card and plays their whole hand out before you make a single decision on yours. See the number you need to beat, then hit, stand, double, or split with full information. Five reversal calls per shoe, dealer stands on all 17s, no insurance, blackjack pays 3 to 1.

Half-Bank Mode

The same rules, one big twist: every bet is automatically forced to half of whatever's left in your bankroll, rounded so a double is always in reach. There's no easing off the throttle — you're either building a run or bankrupt fast. A full shoe plays out to the last card, and the monthly leaderboard tracks who can survive the swings the longest.

Table VII Tournament

Live, in-person blackjack, but nobody's playing the house — you're playing each other. Up to seven seats at a table, escalating blinds on a countdown clock, and a dealer button that rotates so someone at the table reveals one dealer card each round. Biggest bank when the tournament ends takes the top spots. Built to run on real felt with real chips, not a screen.

Table VII Poker

The companion for a live poker night. Real cards get dealt at the real table — this just keeps everything else honest: a blind clock that steps up on schedule, a chip tracker for every player's stack, quick eliminations when someone's felted, and a payout calculator that splits the pool the moment the last hand's done.
