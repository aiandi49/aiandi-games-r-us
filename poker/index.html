House Rules — Games R Us Poker Night

No-limit Texas Hold'em, six-handed — you against five house opponents. Blinds post automatically and escalate on the clock you set in Settings. Each hand deals two hole cards to everyone, then a flop, turn, and river, with a full round of betting after each. Raise with the slider or the quick buttons, or go all-in any time. Side pots are handled automatically when someone's covered. Busted players are out; the night ends when only one stack is left standing, and the payout splits according to the places you configured.
